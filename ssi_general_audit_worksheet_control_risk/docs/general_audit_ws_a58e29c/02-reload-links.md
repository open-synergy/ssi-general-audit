# Reload Links — Control Risk

> **Module:** `ssi_general_audit_worksheet_control_risk`\
> **Model:** `general_audit_ws_a58e29c`\
> **Menu:** Risk Assessment > Control Risk > Control Risk\
> **Actor:** user in group _Control Risk (a58e29c) — User_

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** Status is **On Progress** (`open`).
- **Data:** Control Risk - Entity Level, Control Risk - Cycle Level, or Significant
  Account worksheets exist for the same General Audit.
- **Access:** User is in group _Control Risk (a58e29c) — User_ or higher.

## Flow

1. Open the **Risk Assessment > Control Risk > Control Risk** menu. If **Control Risk**
   is not visible in the top menu bar, click the **+** button at its right end first.
2. Open the worksheet to work on.
3. Open the **Activities** tab.
4. Click the **Reload** button. The button is only visible while the status is **On
   Progress**.

## Post-Condition

- The **Control Risk - Entity Level**, **Control Risk - Cycle Level**, and **Significant
  Account** sections show the worksheets of the same General Audit that currently exist.
- Clicking **Reload** again does not duplicate entries.
