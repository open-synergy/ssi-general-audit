# Reload Links — Acceptance and Continuance of Client Relationships Analysis

> **Module:** `ssi_general_audit_worksheet_acceptance_continuance`\
> **Model:** `general_audit_ws_806c4e1`\
> **Menu:** Pre-Engagement > Client Acceptance > Acceptance and Continuance of Client
> Relationships Analysis\
> **Actor:** user in group _Acceptance and Continuance of Client Relationships Analysis (806c4e1)
> — User_

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** Status is **On Progress** (`open`).
- **Data:** none required; the button only refreshes references to sibling worksheets of
  the same General Audit.
- **Access:** User is in group _Acceptance and Continuance of Client Relationships
  Analysis (806c4e1) — User_ (or higher).

## Flow

1. Open the **Pre-Engagement > Client Acceptance > Acceptance and Continuance of Client
   Relationships Analysis** menu.
2. Open the worksheet to work on.
3. Open the **Links** tab.
4. Click the **Reload** button. The button is only visible while the status is **On
   Progress**.

## Post-Condition

- The **Links** tab shows the worksheet(s) of the same General Audit for: Previous
  Financial Reporting Issues, Management Integrity, Competency/Availability/Independency
  of the assignment team, and Communication With Previous Auditor.
- The risk level of each linked worksheet is shown next to it.
- All other tabs are unaffected.
