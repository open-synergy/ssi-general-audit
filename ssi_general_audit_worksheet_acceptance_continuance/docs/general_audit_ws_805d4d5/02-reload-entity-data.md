# Reload Entity Data — Know Your Customer Principles

> **Module:** `ssi_general_audit_worksheet_acceptance_continuance`\
> **Model:** `general_audit_ws_805d4d5`\
> **Menu:** Pre-Engagement > Client Acceptance > Management Integrity > Know Your
> Customer Principles\
> **Actor:** user in group _Know Your Customer Principles (805d4d5) — User_

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** Status is **On Progress** (`open`).
- **Data:** The General Audit has a client (partner) whose entity data is maintained.
- **Access:** User is in group _Know Your Customer Principles (805d4d5) — User_ (or
  higher).

## Flow

1. Open the **Pre-Engagement > Client Acceptance > Management Integrity > Know Your
   Customer Principles** menu.
2. Open the worksheet to work on.
3. Open the first PMPJ section shown on the form (**SIMPLIFIED**, **INTERMEDIATE**, or
   **ENHANCED**).
4. Click the **Reload** button next to **A. Basic Information of the Customer**. The
   button is only visible while the status is **On Progress**.

## Post-Condition

- The basic information of the customer (entity type, industry, tax number, phone, legal
  and location address) is refreshed from the client record of the General Audit.
- The checklist and the other PMPJ fields are unaffected.
