# Reload Links — Know Your Customer Principles

> **Module:** `ssi_general_audit_worksheet_acceptance_continuance`\
> **Model:** `general_audit_ws_805d4d5`\
> **Menu:** Pre-Engagement > Client Acceptance > Management Integrity > Know Your
> Customer Principles\
> **Actor:** user in group _Know Your Customer Principles (805d4d5) — User_

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** Status is **On Progress** (`open`).
- **Data:** none required; the button only refreshes references to sibling worksheets of
  the same General Audit.
- **Access:** User is in group _Know Your Customer Principles (805d4d5) — User_ (or
  higher).

## Flow

1. Open the **Pre-Engagement > Client Acceptance > Management Integrity > Know Your
   Customer Principles** menu.
2. Open the worksheet to work on.
3. Open the **Links** tab.
4. Click the **Reload** button. The button is only visible while the status is **On
   Progress**.

## Post-Condition

- The **Links** tab shows the worksheet(s) of the same General Audit for: Money
  Laundring Issues.
- The PMPJ risk category taken from the linked worksheet decides which PMPJ sections
  (**Simplified**, **Intermediate**, **Enhanced**) are shown on the form.
- All other tabs are unaffected.
