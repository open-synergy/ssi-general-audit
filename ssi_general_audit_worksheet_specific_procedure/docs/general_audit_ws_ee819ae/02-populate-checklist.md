# Populate Checklist - Commitment and Contingent

> **Module:** `ssi_general_audit_worksheet_specific_procedure`\
> **Model:** `general_audit_ws_ee819ae`\
> **Menu:** Risk Responses > Specific Procedures > Commitment and Contingent\
> **Actor:** user in group _Commitment and Contingent (ee819ae) — User_

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** Status is **On Progress** (`open`).
- **Data:** At least one checklist item exists in the item master of this worksheet.
- **Access:** User is in group _Commitment and Contingent (ee819ae) — User_ or higher.

## Flow

1. Open the **Risk Responses > Specific Procedures > Commitment and Contingent** menu.
   If **Specific Procedures** is not visible in the top menu bar, click the **+** button
   at its right end first.
2. Open the worksheet to work on.
3. Open the **Checklist** tab.
4. Click the **Populate** button. The button is only visible while the status is **On
   Progress**.

## Post-Condition

- The tab has one line for every checklist item in the master; lines that already exist
  are kept with the answer already entered.
- Lines whose item is no longer in the master are removed.
- Clicking the button again does not duplicate lines.
