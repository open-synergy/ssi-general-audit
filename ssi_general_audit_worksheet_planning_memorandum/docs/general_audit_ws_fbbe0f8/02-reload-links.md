# Reload Links — Audit Planning Memorandum - Details

> **Module:** `ssi_general_audit_worksheet_planning_memorandum`\
> **Model:** `general_audit_ws_fbbe0f8`\
> **Menu:** Risk Assessment > Audit Planning Memorandum > Audit Planning Memorandum -
> Details\
> **Actor:** user in group _Audit Planning Memorandum - Detail (fbbe0f8) — User_

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** Status is **On Progress** (`open`).
- **Data:** none required; the button only refreshes references to sibling worksheets of
  the same General Audit.
- **Access:** User is in group _Audit Planning Memorandum - Detail (fbbe0f8) — User_ (or
  higher).

## Flow

1. Open the **Risk Assessment > Audit Planning Memorandum > Audit Planning Memorandum -
   Details** menu. If **Audit Planning Memorandum** is not visible in the top menu bar,
   click the **+** button at its right end first.
2. Open the worksheet to work on.
3. Click the **Reload** button above the tabs. The button is only visible while the
   status is **On Progress**.
4. Open the **Links** tab to see the result.

## Post-Condition

- The **Links** tab shows the worksheets of the same General Audit that this memorandum
  summarises (for example business environment, preliminary materiality and
  understanding of the entity).
- All other tabs are unaffected.
