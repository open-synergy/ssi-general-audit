# Fill Draft Audit Opinion — Proposed Audit Opinion (fc75636)

> **Module:** `ssi_general_audit_worksheet_review`
>
> **Model:** `general_audit_ws_fc75636`
>
> **Menu:** Windup & Reporting > Final Report > Proposed Audit Opinion
>
> **Actor:** user in group _Independen Auditor Report (fc75636) — User_
>
> **State:** open

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** Status is **On Progress**.
- **Access:** User is in group _Independen Auditor Report (fc75636) — User_ (or higher).

## Flow

1. Open the **Windup & Reporting > Final Report > Proposed Audit Opinion** menu.
2. Open the worksheet to draft the audit opinion for.
3. Open the **Draft Audit Opinion** tab.
4. Fill in the nine narrative fields. All are optional, and can be edited or left as
   they are:
   - **Opinion**: Draft narrative of the Opinion paragraph. Pre-filled with a generic
     template; adjust to the engagement.
   - **Basis for Opinion**: Draft narrative of the Basis for Opinion paragraph.
     Pre-filled with a generic template; adjust to the engagement.
   - **Key Audit Matters**: Draft narrative of the Key Audit Matters paragraph. Empty by
     default — only fill in for engagements that require this section.
   - **Other Information**: Draft narrative of the Other Information paragraph. Empty by
     default — only fill in when the engagement includes other information.
   - **Responsibilities of Management**: Draft narrative of the Responsibilities of
     Management paragraph. Pre-filled with a generic template; adjust to the engagement.
   - **Auditor's Responsibilities**: Draft narrative of the Auditor's Responsibilities
     paragraph. Pre-filled with a generic template; adjust to the engagement.
   - **Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements**: Draft narrative of this
     section. Empty by default — only fill in when such requirements apply.
   - **Emphasis of Matter**: Draft narrative of the Emphasis of Matter paragraph. Empty
     by default — only fill in when this paragraph is needed.
   - **Other Matter**: Draft narrative of the Other Matter paragraph. Empty by default —
     only fill in when this paragraph is needed.
5. Click **Save**.

## Post-Condition

- The nine Draft Audit Opinion fields keep the values entered, including any HTML markup
  (bold, bullet lists, etc.) used while editing.
- The **Checklist** and **Links** tabs are unaffected.
