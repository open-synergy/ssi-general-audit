# Reload Links — Understanding of The Entity and it's Environment

> **Module:** `ssi_general_audit_worksheet_understanding_entity`\
> **Model:** `general_audit_ws_f87b2e1`\
> **Menu:** Risk Assessment > Understanding The Entity > Understanding of The Entity and
> it's Environment\
> **Actor:** user in group _Understanding of The Entity and it's Environment (f87b2e1) —
> User_

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** Status is **On Progress** (`open`).
- **Data:** none required; the button only refreshes references to sibling worksheets of
  the same General Audit.
- **Access:** User is in group _Understanding of The Entity and it's Environment
  (f87b2e1) — User_ (or higher).

## Flow

1. Open the **Risk Assessment > Understanding The Entity > Understanding of The Entity
   and it's Environment** menu.
2. Open the worksheet to work on.
3. Open the **Activities** tab.
4. Click the **Reload** button. The button is only visible while the status is **On
   Progress**.

## Post-Condition

- The **Activities** tab shows the worksheet(s) of the same General Audit for: General
  Information and Legal Aspect, Structure Organization and Responsibilities, Main
  Business Activity Process, Business Cycle Summaries, Understanding of the Business
  Environment, Understanding of preparation of Financial Statements, Understanding of
  Relevant Regulations, and Going Concern Analysis.
- The state and conclusion of each linked worksheet are shown next to it.
- All other tabs are unaffected.
